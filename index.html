Welcome to Blackout City Roleplay

Server Rules & Regulations

1. OOC: Out of Character chat must not be used to share or request HRP/OOC information. 2. RDM (Random Deathmatch): Killing any player without a valid RP reason is strictly prohibited. 3. VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch): Using a vehicle as a weapon to run over or kill players is forbidden. 4. Value of Life (fearRP): You must always value and protect your character's life in dangerous situations. 5. Power Gaming: Performing actions that are physically impossible or giving yourself unfair advantages (e.g., ignoring heavy injuries after a crash). 6. Meta Gaming: Using outside information (Discord, streams) that your character did not acquire in-game. All major actions require a video recording. 7. Fail RP: Actions that contradict realistic roleplay behavior or break the immersion of the city. 8. Force RP: Forcing another player into a situation without giving them a fair chance to react or defend themselves. 9. Win RP: Prioritizing "winning" a scenario over good roleplay or inventing rules/items that do not exist in-game. 10. Combat Logging: Leaving the game or going AFK to avoid an active roleplay scenario. 11. Combat Storing: Fleeing into a Greenzone during an active gunfight or chase. 12. Clear Shot: There is no such thing as a "clear shot" exemption when a hostage's life is at risk. 13. Revenge Kill: Returning to exact revenge after dying requires a 2-hour cooldown, proper RP background, and video proof. 14. Disrespect / Toxicity: Insulting or mocking a downed player via voice or emotes is strictly banned. 15. Rule Abuse: Exploiting specific rules or greenzones to provoke players safely. 16. Pain RP: Reacting realistically to injuries, crashes, and medical events as you would in real life. 17. Severe Language: Blasphemy or insulting family members will result in immediate bans if reported. 18. Glitch Abuse: Finding and exploiting bugs without reporting them to the staff team results in a permanent ban. 19. Cheating / Mods: Using third-party cheats or modifications that give visual or mechanical advantages results in a permanent ban. 20. Sexual Harassment: Any form of sexual harassment or non-consensual scenarios will lead to severe permanent sanctions. 21. Skipping Scenarios: Refusing to complete a scene with EMS, Police, or legal staff is prohibited. 22. Realistic Names: Characters must have realistic first and last names. 23. Free Looting: Stealing items from third parties without proper context is forbidden. 25. Asset Transfer: Transferring assets to another player before a character death (perma) or server leave is forbidden. 26. New Life Rule (NLR): Following a perma-death, a new character must have no relation to the old one. 27. Reporting Issues: Complete your roleplay scenes first, then report issues to staff later using appropriate commands. 28. Refunds: All refund requests require valid video proof (recording). 29. /me Command: Used to describe a physical feeling or expression. 30. /do Command: Used to describe environmental actions or clarify physical details. 31. Mass RP: Always assume the city is populated; never act as if areas are completely empty. 32. Death Rules: Faint/punch deaths allow memory recall; melee weapon deaths require a 12-hour memory loss; gun deaths cause total scene memory loss. 33. Respecting Female Players: Harassment, toxic behavior, or inappropriate language directed at or around female players is strictly prohibited. 34. Quality Roleplay: Poor quality roleplay, toxicity, griefing, or trying to ruin other players' experiences leads to unwhitelisting or permanent bans. 35. Emotes in Combat: Using custom animations or emotes during active fights is strictly forbidden. 37. Stunt Driving: Unrealistic vehicle jumps or motorcycle stunts resulting in power-gaming are banned. 38. Fail Driving: Driving vehicles with broken tires or scaling unpaved mountains with standard cars is forbidden. 39. Mix RP: Bringing out-of-character (Discord) arguments into the game world or vice versa is punishable by bans. 40. License Plates: Vehicles without license plates cannot be easily tracked; police can impound them for investigation.
